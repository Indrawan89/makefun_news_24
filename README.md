A one-day world news poster for make.fun's News image bounty (24 November 2026, $100 in USDC).

`main.py` (pure Python standard library, no network) draws a 1024×1024 newspaper poster — masthead, lead story (Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang), a one-day market bar, two feature panels (War at sea, Sputnik-S), and 33 dated stories from 7 regions plus Also today — and writes it to `output/entry.png`.

Deterministic: two runs write byte-identical PNGs (30,201 bytes).
